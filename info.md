# Staykey

Connect Home Assistant to Staykey for short-term rental automation. Stream Z-Wave, Zigbee, and Matter smart-lock and device events to Staykey, and let Staykey drive guest access codes, climate, and turnovers for your Airbnb and VRBO properties — local-first, no vendor cloud lock-in.

## Features
- Auto-discovers your locks, thermostats, covers, and switches
- Gateway mode (persistent, bidirectional) with offline buffering
- Works with Z-Wave JS and Matter locks
- UI config flow — no YAML required
